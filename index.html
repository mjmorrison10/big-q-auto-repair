Our Services

Complete Auto Repair &
Maintenance Under One Roof

From a quick oil change to a full engine rebuild, Big Q handles it all with skill and precision. No job too big or too small. We explain your options clearly before we turn a single wrench.

Oil Change & Lube

When was your last oil change? If you can't remember, it's been too long. We use premium synthetic and conventional oils and only install what your car actually needs.

Brake Service & Repair

Squeaking, grinding, or a soft pedal? We've seen it all. Our thorough brake diagnostics pinpoint the exact problem. No scare tactics. Just honest, safe repairs.

Tire Sales & Alignment

Competitive pricing on all major tire brands, plus precision wheel alignments that extend tire life and boost fuel economy. We price-match any legitimate competitor, guaranteed.

Engine Repair & Rebuilds

Don't ignore that check engine light. Our ASE-certified technicians handle everything from sensor replacements to complete rebuilds. We diagnose it right the first time.

Transmission Service

Hard shifts, slipping, or leaks? Before you spend thousands at the dealer, let us give you an honest assessment. We service both automatic and manual transmissions.

Electrical & Battery

Dead battery at 7AM? Faulty alternator leaving you stranded? We trace the root cause, explain it in plain English, and fix it right. No guesswork, just results.

Suspension & Steering

Bumpy rides and uneven tire wear aren't just annoying, they're dangerous. We diagnose and repair shocks, struts, tie rods, and power steering to keep you safe.

Exhaust & Muffler

Loud exhaust? Failed emissions? Rusted-out pipes? We repair and replace exhaust components to keep your car running clean and quiet. Pass emissions with confidence.
